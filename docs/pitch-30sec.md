# 30-Second Pitch — VoiceBridge SD

Practice this at dinner (Sat 7:30pm) until it fits in 30 seconds. This is also your
opener at the judging table before the live demo.

## The pitch (say it out loud, time yourself)

"Finding free food or shelter in San Diego means digging through five websites,
in English, on a phone. If you can't read English well, or you can't see the
screen, that search is the barrier. So I built VoiceBridge SD: you speak what
you need — 'I need free food near La Jolla today' — and it answers out loud,
in plain language, with the name, address, and hours. It works in Spanish too.
Want to try it yourself?"

> Keep the Spanish line only if the Spanish test passes tonight. In the
> how-it-works answer, say "Bright Data scrapes live listings" only if
> the scrape has succeeded; otherwise say "Bright Data is our scraper —
> the demo runs on real listings loaded into Neon."

## If they ask "how does it work?" (15 more seconds)

"Bright Data scrapes live listings, Neon stores them, Gemini understands your
question and writes a simple summary, and ElevenLabs speaks it. The voice IS
the product — it's for people the text web leaves behind."

## Delivery tips

- Smile, slow down on the problem sentence — that's the part judges remember.
- End on "Want to try it yourself?" and hand them the mic (Chrome, 🎤 Speak
  button). A judge speaking their own question is worth more than any slide.
- If the demo glitches, say so out loud and show the fallback: the answer text
  is still on screen, and the pre-recorded audio still plays. Judges love an
  honest recovery more than a perfect take.
- Never claim anything you haven't seen work: live scrape only if the scrape
  succeeded, live Gemini only if a Gemini call returned, Grok Bot only if it
  is actually set up. The docs/devpost-draft.md status header lists what's
  confirmed right now.
